First Steps - original MoveBeat tutorial

18 seconds, 120 BPM, mono Ogg Vorbis at 48 kHz.
Four count-in beats, then 32 alternating left/right notes.
The first eight notes accept any cut direction; later notes alternate down/up.

Music and chart are generated by tools/generate_tutorial.py from oscillators
and deterministic noise. No third-party recordings or melodies are included.
